Common Questions

Theater seat reupholstery FAQ

Is it cheaper to reupholster or replace theater seats?

Reupholstering or re-covering existing seats typically runs around $100 per seat versus $500+ to buy and install new chairs — often 70–80% savings — while keeping your existing seat frames and layout.

Will my theater have to close during the work?

No. Work is phased overnight or one screen at a time so the rest of your screens keep running. We schedule around your showtimes.

What materials do you use?

Commercial-grade, fire-rated vinyl and fabrics built for high-traffic public seating and code compliance — not residential upholstery.

Can you handle recliners and VIP seating?

Yes. We recover premium recliners, loungers, and dine-in seating in addition to standard fixed cinema seats.
