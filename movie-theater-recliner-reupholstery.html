Common Questions

Recliner reupholstery FAQ

Can you reupholster powered cinema recliners?

Yes — we recover the worn surfaces (backs, seats, arms, and chaise) while leaving the recline mechanism and frame in place, so the seat works exactly as before but looks new.

Is recliner reupholstery cheaper than replacing them?

Almost always. Powered recliners are expensive to replace; recovering the worn material is a fraction of the cost and keeps your existing layout and electronics.

What materials do you use on recliners?

Commercial-grade, fire-rated vinyl and fabric built for high-traffic premium seating — durable enough for constant recline use and code-compliant.

Will my VIP theater have to close?

No — work is phased overnight or screen-by-screen so the rest of your screens keep running.
